## Brand availability and denominations

- Brand list and detail responses include `available`. A temporary outage leaves the brand visible with `available: false`.
- The brands list includes `fund_currencyisocode` and `variable_price`, plus `allowed_prices_in_cents` for fixed-price brands or `min_price_in_cents` and `max_price_in_cents` for variable-price brands.
- `description`, `regions`, and `embeddable` remain exclusive to single-brand responses.
- New orders that explicitly select an unavailable brand return HTTP `422` with the existing `ERROR_CAMPAIGN_INVALID_BRAND` response. Full Catalog orders require at least one available brand supporting the region and amount.

See the [brands reference](/api-reference/brands/list-brands) and [unavailable-brand handling](/guides/response-codes#unavailable-brands).
